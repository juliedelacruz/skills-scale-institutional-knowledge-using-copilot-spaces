# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define and execute quality assurance strategies. They own test planning, acceptance validation, and quality metrics to ensure features meet acceptance criteria and user expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Design test plans and define acceptance criteria alignment
- Execute and oversee manual and automated testing
- Identify and triage quality issues
- Collaborate with developers on testability and Definition of Done
- Report quality metrics and risks

### Goals
- Ensure all features meet acceptance criteria before release
- Reduce bugs in production through thorough testing
- Enable fast, confident releases

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning (input on test scope)
- Execution sync (quality status updates)
- Release readiness review
- Collaboration with Product Managers on acceptance criteria

---

## Technical Lead / Solution Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide architectural guidance, design oversight, and technical problem-solving. They ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with system architecture.

### Responsibilities
- Review technical designs and propose solutions to complex problems
- Guide technology choices and trade-offs
- Mentor developers and conduct architecture reviews
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Ensure compliance with coding standards and architectural patterns

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, maintainable solutions
- Minimize technical debt
- Enable team growth through mentorship

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review and feedback
- Technical risk discussions in planning
- Collaboration with Project Managers on technical risk assessment

---

## Sponsor / Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Sponsors represent business interests and provide funding, priorities, and strategic context. They make go/no-go decisions and escalate as needed.

### Responsibilities
- Define business requirements and success metrics
- Provide project funding and prioritization input
- Approve major decisions and scope changes
- Monitor project health and escalate issues
- Communicate business impact to leadership

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver business value
- Minimize project risk to the organization
- Maintain stakeholder alignment and confidence

### Typical Communication
- Project approval and sponsorship
- Monthly stakeholder updates
- Escalation and decision gates
- Collaboration with Project Managers and Product Leads on business context

---

## Scrum Master / Iteration Lead

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters or Iteration Leads facilitate agile ceremonies, remove impediments, and coach teams on iterative delivery practices.

### Responsibilities
- Schedule and facilitate sprint planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and help resolve team impediments
- Coach team on agile principles and ceremonies
- Shield team from external distractions
- Track sprint metrics and team health signals

### Goals
- Enable the team to work efficiently and predictably
- Foster psychological safety and continuous improvement
- Improve iteration predictability and cycle time

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- One-on-ones with team members
- Escalation of blockers
- Collaboration with Project Managers on team health and process improvement

---

## DevOps / Release Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps and Release Engineers manage infrastructure, CI/CD pipelines, and release processes. They enable safe, reliable deployments to production.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines
- Manage infrastructure and deployment environments
- Coordinate release deployments
- Monitor system health and troubleshoot production issues
- Document runbooks and incident playbooks

### Goals
- Enable fast, reliable deployments with minimal risk
- Maintain high system availability and performance
- Reduce mean time to recovery (MTTR) for incidents

### Typical Communication
- Pre-release reviews and deployment planning
- Incident response and post-mortems
- Infrastructure and performance discussions
- Collaboration with Project Managers on release coordination and with QA on deployment readiness
